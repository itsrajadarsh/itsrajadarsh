## About Me

I completed my **B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering** from the **Central University of Haryana** in **2025**, with a CGPA of **8.15/10.0**, specializing in software engineering, data structures, algorithms, and full-stack web development.<br>  
I’m a passionate **Software Developer** who thrives on solving real-world problems and building scalable, user-centric solutions.

With a strong foundation in **C++**, **JavaScript**, and **Full-Stack Development**, I specialize in embedded systems (STM32, Qt) and modern web technologies (React, Node.js, PostgreSQL). I love taking on new challenges, optimizing systems, and exploring emerging technologies.<br>  
My journey has given me hands-on experience in **full-stack development**, embedded systems, and secure applications, allowing me to build dynamic, responsive, and scalable solutions.

Currently, I'm working as a **Deputy Software Engineer** at **ZOSH Aerospace** in Bengaluru, where I develop GUI communication interfaces (C++/Qt), embedded software on STM32, and automation tools with AutoHotkey.
